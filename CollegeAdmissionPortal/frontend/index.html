Steps for Centralized Admission Workflow

Follow the streamlined centralized admission workflow from application to final confirmation

  1. Step 1 - Account Registration & Login: Create your candidate account and log in.
  2. Step 2 - Academic Scores & Category: Enter your 10th SSC, 12th HSC marks, and MHT-CET percentile.
  3. Step 3 - Mandatory Document Upload: Upload clear scans of your Aadhaar Card, SSC, HSC, and CET marksheets (PDF/JPG/PNG).
  4. Step 4 - Branch Preference Ordering: Arrange preferred engineering branches in priority order (1st to 6th) and submit your application.
  5. Step 5 - Admin Verification & Branch Allocation: The college admission committee reviews credentials and allocates an available engineering branch based on merit.
  6. Step 6 - Student Branch Acceptance: Review the allocated seat, admin remarks, and explicitly Accept to confirm admission.
